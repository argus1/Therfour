# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to creating a welcoming and inclusive community for the Therfour project. As contributors and maintainers, we pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, color, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, political belief, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or any other dimension of identity.

We are dedicated to building an environment where diverse perspectives on AI, voice technology, and open-source development are valued and respected.

## Our Standards

### Examples of Behavior That Contributes to a Positive Environment

- Using inclusive and welcoming language
- Being respectful of differing opinions on technical approaches and design decisions
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism and feedback on code and ideas
- Focusing on what is best for the community and project
- Showing empathy and patience toward other community members, especially those new to the Therfour codebase or voice/AI technology in general
- Actively helping others debug issues and understand the complex systems we're building (telephony, TTS, STT, LLM integration)

### Examples of Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or threats directed at any community member
- Sexualized language, jokes, imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Personal, political, or ad hominem attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (such as physical or email addresses) without explicit permission
- Deliberately derailing discussions or pull request reviews
- Dismissing concerns or feedback based on someone's demographic characteristics
- Unwelcome advanced on anyone in the community

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers and moderators are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to:

- Remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, and issues that violate this Code of Conduct
- Temporarily or permanently ban any contributor whose behavior is deemed inappropriate, threatening, hostile, or harmful
- Make enforcement decisions with fairness and consistency

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies:

- Within all Therfour project spaces, including repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, and documentation
- In public spaces when an individual is officially representing the project (using an official email address, social media account, or acting as a project representative at events or conferences)
- In one-on-one communications related to the project

## Reporting and Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by contacting the project maintainers through the GitHub issue tracker or by sending a private message to a maintainer. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and confidentially.

We are committed to:

- Responding to reports within 48 hours
- Investigating all complaints fairly and thoroughly
- Protecting the privacy and safety of reporters
- Taking appropriate corrective action based on investigation findings

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1, and is tailored for the Therfour AI Voice Platform community.

## Questions?

If you have questions about this Code of Conduct, feel free to open an issue or discuss with the maintainers. We're here to help create a positive community.
