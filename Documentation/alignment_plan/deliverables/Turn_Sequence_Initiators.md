# Turn Sequence Initiators

Date: 2026-05-01
Source grounding: Chroma benchmark corpus themes (confidential helpline support, harm reduction, overdose safety)

Each initiator is designed for a first call turn, under 20 seconds at normal speaking pace.

1. "Hi, this is Terris. I am here to offer confidential, non-judgmental support. What is your name?"

2. "Hello, you have reached Terris. I can help with safer-use and harm-reduction questions. What should I call you?"

3. "Hi, I am Terris. Thanks for calling. Before we begin, may I ask your name?"

4. "This is Terris. I am here to talk through safety and support options with you. What is your name?"

5. "Hello, I am Terris. We can go at your pace, and this is a confidential space. What is your name?"
