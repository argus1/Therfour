# Turn Sequence Terminators

Date: 2026-05-01
Source grounding: Chroma benchmark corpus themes (confidential helpline support, non-judgmental harm-reduction care, safety-focused follow-up)

Each terminator is designed for a final call turn around 25 seconds at normal speaking pace.

1. "Thank you for reaching out today, {name}. I really appreciate the trust you showed in this call, and I want you to know you are always welcome to call back anytime you want support, information, or just someone steady to talk with."

2. "{name}, thank you for calling and for sharing so openly with me today. If anything shifts later, or if you simply want to talk things through again, please call back anytime because we are here to support you without judgment."

3. "I am really glad you called today, {name}, and thank you for taking this step to reach out. You are always welcome to call back whenever you need confidential, non-judgmental harm-reduction support, including if you just need help sorting out your next safe step."

4. "Thank you for reaching out, {name}. You did something important by calling today, and I hope you remember that you do not have to carry this alone. Please call back anytime you want more support, guidance, or a calmer conversation about staying safe."

5. "{name}, thank you for connecting with us today and giving yourself this moment of care. If you need harm-reduction support again, or even if you just want to check in, please call back anytime because you are always welcome here."
